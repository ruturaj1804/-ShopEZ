## Empathy Map Analysis for Shoppers & Sellers

| Quadrant | Customer / Shopper Perspective | Merchant / Seller Perspective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Who are we empathizing with?** | Retail consumers purchasing lifestyle, fashion, and electronic products | Independent merchants and store administrators managing catalogs and orders |
| **What do they need to DO?** | Browse catalog, filter categories, inspect product details, add to cart, and complete orders via Cash on Delivery (COD) | Add products with image uploads, update prices/quantities on the fly, and fulfill customer shipments promptly |
| **What do they SEE?** | Responsive product cards with discount tags, star ratings, stock availability badges, and an interactive shopping cart | Seller dashboard showing revenue cards, total orders, product inventory tables, and low-stock warning banners |
| **What do they SAY?** | *"I want clear specifications, genuine ratings, and immediate confirmation after placing my order."* | *"I need to change prices and stock without navigating through 5 different configuration screens."* |
| **What do they DO?** | Filter by category ('Electronics', 'Clothing', 'Footwear'), search keywords, manage cart items, and track order statuses in profile | Upload product images via Multer, use inline editing on the seller dashboard, and mark orders as Shipped/Delivered |
| **What do they HEAR?** | Peers recommending clean e-commerce platforms with honest pricing and easy navigation | Customers expecting prompt order dispatch and transparent delivery timelines |
| **PAINS (Frustrations)** | Slow loading times, complicated checkouts, hidden taxes, and lack of clarity on whether an item is in stock | Overselling items when stock runs out, cumbersome image uploading, and lack of consolidated revenue metrics |
| **GAINS (Desires & Goals)** | Instant client-side navigation (Vite + React Router), transparent pricing, one-click cart management, and order history tracking | Dedicated `/admin/sell` dashboard, automated low-stock warnings (< 10 units), and inline inventory management |
