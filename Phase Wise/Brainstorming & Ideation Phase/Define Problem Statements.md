## Customer Problem Statement Template
A well-articulated customer problem statement allows the team to pinpoint friction in product discovery, purchasing, and store management to build an e-commerce platform customers and merchants love.

| Problem Statement | I am (Customer / User) | I'm trying to | But | Because | Which makes me feel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PS-1: Online Shopper (Discovery & Checkout)** | An active consumer looking for lifestyle and electronic products | Find authentic products quickly, inspect customer reviews, and checkout with zero friction | Most platforms are cluttered with ads, require lengthy account creation before browsing, and have slow checkout flows | Traditional monolith stores lack modern Single Page Application (SPA) responsiveness and dynamic cart updates | Frustrated and impatient, leading to cart abandonment |
| **PS-2: Seller / Merchant (Catalog & Stock Control)** | A small business seller listing items on ShopEZ | Upload product listings with images, adjust prices and stock counts dynamically, and receive low-stock alerts | Existing seller dashboards are bloated, require multiple page reloads to edit a price, and fail to warn before running out of stock | Traditional admin portals lack inline editing and automated inventory threshold alerts (< 10 units) | Overwhelmed by operational overhead and anxious about overselling out-of-stock items |
| **PS-3: Platform Admin (Order Governance & Metrics)** | The store operations administrator | Monitor gross sales revenue, manage registered users, and update multi-stage order fulfillment statuses (Processing -> Shipped -> Delivered) | Order updates and customer records are scattered across disconnected tools without consolidated analytics | Lack of a unified MERN centralized administration dashboard with role-based access control | Concerned about delayed order fulfillment and lack of operational transparency |
